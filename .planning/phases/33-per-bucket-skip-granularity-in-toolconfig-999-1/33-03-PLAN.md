- Self-tests pinning collect-only absence of skipped-bucket tests AND presence of non-skipped buckets; at least one subprocess-based collect-only test is mandatory (W3 fix); a synthetic-Metafunc unit test sits alongside for fast feedback.

           owning_bucket: BucketName | None = None
           for bucket_name, fn_set in TEST_FUNCTION_BUCKETS.items():

                   owning_bucket = bucket_name  # type: ignore[assignment]
